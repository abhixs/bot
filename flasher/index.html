RoboThings S3 Mini

Xiaozhi AI voice assistant for ESP32-S3 Super Mini Zero, with animated robot eyes and voice alarms.

Flash from the browser

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer and a USB data cable.
  2. If the port does not show up: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT.
  3. Click the button and pick the serial port.

This browser cannot flash. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https or localhost.

Manual flashing (ESP32-S3, 4MB, DIO, 80MHz): bootloader.bin @ 0x0, partition-table.bin @ 0x8000, ota_data_initial.bin @ 0xd000, xiaozhi.bin @ 0x10000, generated_assets.bin @ 0x300000. Or merged-binary.bin @ 0x0.

Try saying

  • "Wake me up at 7 am" / "Kal subah 7 baje alarm laga do"
  • "Set an alarm for 6:30 every weekday and turn on the lamp"
  • "10 minute ka timer lagao"
  • "Which alarms are set?" / "Cancel the 7 o'clock alarm"
  • "Turn on the lamp"

Any button stops a ringing alarm. You can also say "snooze for 5 minutes".
